Oncology Esthetics

Skincare that honors
what you're going through

Not every esthetician is trained to work safely with clients undergoing cancer treatment. Stephanie holds a formal oncology esthetics certification — which means your skin is never put at risk, and your comfort is always the priority.

Treatments are adapted based on your specific protocol, current medications, and skin sensitivity. You deserve to feel cared for, even when your body is fighting hard.

  • Safe for clients in active treatment
  • Adapted protocols for radiation and chemotherapy effects
  • Gentle, non-reactive product selection
  • A space where you are seen, not just treated
